# NNNN — Title in decision form

Status: Accepted | Superseded by NNNN | Rejected (YYYY-MM, change or commit)

## Context

The forces at play: what problem, what constraints, what triggered the
decision. Two to six sentences; link the openspec change for the full
process record.

## Decision

The rule, stated actively and precisely enough to enforce. Name the
modules/formats it binds (`src/...`, format versions). Keep code snippets
only when the shape itself is the decision.

## Consequences

What this commits future changes to; costs accepted; what becomes easier
or impossible. This section is the reason the ADR exists — be concrete.

## Rejected alternatives

One bullet per alternative and the reason it lost. "We considered X and
did Y instead" is the knowledge that evaporates without this file.
